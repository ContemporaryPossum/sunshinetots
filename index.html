Big questions,
simple answers.

Which programme suits my child?+

Sunshine Tots' published childcare programmes serve children from 2 months to 3 years, including infant care, toddler care and Explorer Class. Tadika Si Cahaya's kindergarten programme serves ages 4 to 6. The team can recommend a class for your child's stage.

Can we visit before enrolling?+

Yes. Contact the team on WhatsApp to arrange a visit, meet the teachers and discuss your child's needs. Please confirm your appointment and the correct centre before travelling.

What are the fees and care hours?+

Half-day, extended and full-day options are publicly listed. Fees and availability can vary by age and programme. Ask the team for the current fee schedule, operating hours and any additional charges.

How do the two centres differ?+

Sunshine Tots focuses on care and early growth for younger children. Tadika Si Cahaya (Sunshine Kids Playhouse) focuses on preschool play and learning. Both are in Kota Kemuning; their addresses are shown below.

What does the preschool curriculum cover?+

The school's published learning areas include Cambridge English, Bahasa Melayu, Mandarin, mathematics, science, and moral and character building, alongside sensory play, physical development, creativity and life skills.
